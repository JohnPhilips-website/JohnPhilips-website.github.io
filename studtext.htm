Minnesota State University:

I teach history part-time at the Minnesota State University branch campus in Akita, whenever enough students preregister for my courses.  If you are an MSU student and thinking of taking a class with me, it is important that you preregister.  If enough students do not preregister the class will not be offered.  I found a desperate student outside my door at the beginning of one quarter.  He needed my class but had not preregistered, and the class had been canceled.

I taught survey U.S. history at a U.S. university level (much more demanding than what I teach in Hirosaki) for a total of 6 quarters (the equivalent of 2 years).  Now I am teaching World History.  The first quarter is to 1500 and the second quarter is from 1500.
