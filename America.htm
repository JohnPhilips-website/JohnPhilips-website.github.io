American Studies

Crossroads is the main site for interdisciplinary American Studies. Interroads is a comparative and international American Studies site, especially useful for those of us who teach American Studies overseas. If you want to know more you can check out the Interroads archives, and read the introductory notices.

The H-USA list is one of the H-Net group of Internet mailing lists. I am the current online editor.
